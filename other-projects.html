No. 01 · Electronics repair

Fixing an Xbox Controller

My Xbox controller sat unused for years after its right bumper stopped working. After I started working on the quadcopter, I thought, “I can solder now, and I’m getting into electronics, why not try fixing the controller?” After doing some research, I bought a replacement motherboard and swapped it in, doing the soldering myself on a very questionable setup with no extra light and no extra hands. Once I was done soldering I put it all back together, put the batteries in, loaded up a game, and started playing.

ProblemDead right bumper
FixNew motherboard

Motion-Controlled RC Car
