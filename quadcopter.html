Autonomous Quadcopter

Designed, printed, and configured from the frame up

A 7-inch quadcopter I'm building to learn every layer of an autonomous multirotor: structure, propulsion, power, avionics, and flight software. The airframe is my own design, and the flight controller is set up to fly GPS missions on its own.

Airframe

I designed the frame in SolidWorks around 7-inch propellers, with room for the flight controller, GPS, receiver, and telemetry radio on one stack.

The frame is 3D printed in ASA. I chose ASA over PLA because it handles heat and sunlight much better, which matters for a frame that sits next to warm electronics and flies outdoors.

D

Flight software

The flight controller runs ArduCopter, which I configure and tune through Mission Planner. The build is set up for three autonomous behaviors:

  • Waypoint missions: plan a route on a map in Mission Planner, upload it over telemetry, and the drone flies it.
  • GPS position hold: the drone holds its position in the air without stick input.
  • Return-to-launch failsafe: the drone flies itself home and lands if something goes wrong.
