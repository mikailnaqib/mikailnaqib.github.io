What I built

I modeled and 3D printed every part of this upgrade.

  • Payload capacity: modified the platform so it carries 12% more than before.
  • Mounting platforms: added platforms to the airframe for the lab's new electronics.
  • Electronics housings: designed enclosures that hold and protect the electronics on those platforms.
